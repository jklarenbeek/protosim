Additional limitations to retain in the backlog, from edition 2.0. Near program-memory reads: rune16 uses pgm_read_word, and process_new reads PROGMEM descriptors with near accessors. These are not automatically broken on today's linked image; larger builds must keep near-read objects in reach or use far-access support. [S7, S10, R8] Logger ownership: the rotating error_pool deliberately overwrites older entries while queued events carry pointers to its slots. Bursts can replace the contents of unconsumed records. Size tuning alone is not an ownership guarantee; define loss reporting or stable message ownership. [S7] IPC counters: ipc_pipe_available and ipc_pool_count_free read multi-byte state outside an atomic snapshot. On an 8-bit AVR with concurrent ISR updates, observations may tear.
